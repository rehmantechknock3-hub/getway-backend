Development Milestones Plan
Total Timeline: 16 Weeks Max

Milestone 1 (Project Setup & Platform Foundation)
Timeline: Week 1 – Week 3
Scope:
• Finalize platform structure and overall system planning
• Set up the core platform environment and database
• Implement account registration and login system
• Prepare the base structure for both mobile apps
• Prepare the base structure for the admin dashboard
Deliverables:
• Platform foundation ready for development
• User account system working
• Initial app structure for customer, provider, and admin panels

Milestone 2 (Customer Mobile App (Booking Experience))
Timeline: Week 4 – Week 7
Scope:
• Customer signup and profile management
• Discover nearby service providers on the map
• View provider profiles, ratings, and services
• Save favorite providers
• Create service bookings
• View booking history
• Track booking status from request to completion
Deliverables:
• Working Customer Mobile App
• Provider discovery and booking system

Milestone 3 (Service Provider Mobile App)
Timeline: Week 8 – Week 10
Scope:
• Provider account registration and profile setup
• Upload verification documents and service photos
• Provider dashboard showing upcoming jobs
• Accept or reject booking requests
• Manage availability (online/offline status)
• Navigate to customer locations
• Track earnings and completed services
Deliverables:
• Working Service Provider Mobile App
• Job management and provider dashboard

Milestone 4 (Payments, Messaging & Live Updates)
Timeline: Week 11 – Week 13
Scope:
• Secure in-app payments for bookings
• Automatic platform commission deduction
• Messaging between customer and provider
• Push notifications for booking updates
• Live service status updates
• Location tracking during service
Deliverables:
• Fully connected marketplace experience
• Real-time booking updates and communication

Milestone 5 (Admin Dashboard, Testing & Launch)
Timeline: Week 14 – Week 16
Scope:
• Admin dashboard to manage the platform
• Manage customers and service providers
• Approve provider registrations
• Monitor bookings and platform activity
• Manage platform commission and transactions
• Reports for bookings and revenue
• Full platform testing and improvements
• Deployment to cloud servers
• Preparation for App Store and Play Store release
Deliverables:
• Admin management dashboard
• Fully tested platform ready for launch
• Mobile apps ready for store submission

Final Deliverables:
• Customer Mobile App (iOS & Android)
• Service Provider Mobile App (iOS & Android)
• Admin Web Dashboard
• Backend platform and database
• Cloud deployment
• App Store & Play Store ready builds
